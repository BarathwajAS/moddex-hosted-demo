==========================================================
  MODDEX - INTERACTION SOUNDS
==========================================================

Folder:  C:\ModStudio\assets\sfx\

  click.mp3   your sample - buttons, chips, mod cards
  hover.mp3   your sample, quieter - mouse over a button
  tab.mp3     your sample, pitched down - Upload <-> Studio
  open.mp3    placeholder - popups, settings, image zoom
  close.mp3   placeholder - anything closing, Escape key
  done.mp3    placeholder - a new render finished
  error.mp3   placeholder - generation failed


TO REPLACE ANY SOUND
--------------------
  1. Rename your file to the exact name above (lowercase, .mp3)
  2. Drop it in the folder and overwrite
  3. Refresh ModDex with Ctrl + F5

No code changes. That is the whole process.


WHAT MAKES A SOUND FEEL RIGHT
-----------------------------
  SHORT     clicks under 0.15s, whooshes under 0.4s
  QUIET     hover fires constantly - keep it barely there
  NO GAP    silence at the start of a file feels like lag


VOLUME AND MUTE
---------------
Settings (gear, top right) -> "Interaction sounds"
  On / Off button   mutes everything
  Slider            overall volume
Remembered on that computer, so a demo can be silent.


IF YOU HEAR NOTHING
-------------------
  Click once anywhere on the page. Browsers block all audio
  until you interact with the page - it cannot be turned off.

  Check Settings shows On and the slider is above zero.

  Windows hides file extensions, so check you do not have
  click.mp3.mp3 in the folder.
