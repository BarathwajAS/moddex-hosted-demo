==============================================================
  PUT MODDEX ONLINE - GET A LINK THAT WORKS ANYWHERE
==============================================================

No commands. No installing. Just a web browser.

This puts ModDex on a free computer on the internet, so it works even
when the shop PC is switched off.

About 10 minutes, once.


--------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 - Make a free account
--------------------------------------------------------------
Go to      huggingface.co/join
Sign up. Free. No card needed.


--------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 - Create the space for your tool
--------------------------------------------------------------
Go to      huggingface.co/new-space

Fill in:

  Space name          moddex
  License            leave as is
  Select the SDK      click  Docker    then choose  Blank
  Space hardware      CPU basic - FREE
  Visibility          Private

Choose Private. It means only you can open it after logging in.

Click  Create Space.


--------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 - Upload the tool
--------------------------------------------------------------
On your new space page, click the  Files  tab at the top.

Click  Add file   then   Upload files.

Now unzip ModDex.zip on your computer. Open the ModStudio folder.

Drag EVERYTHING from inside that folder into the browser window.
Folders too - assets, catalog, ui, data.

Important: drag the CONTENTS of the ModStudio folder, not the folder
itself. The Dockerfile must end up at the top level.

At the bottom click  Commit changes to main.

The space now starts building. It takes 2 to 4 minutes. You will see
Building, then Running.


--------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 - Set your staff PIN
--------------------------------------------------------------
Do not skip this.

On the space page click  Settings  (top right).

Scroll to  Variables and secrets.  Click  New secret.

  Name     MODDEX_PIN
  Value    4321          (use your own number)

Click  Save.

Add a second secret the same way:

  Name     MODDEX_DAILY_CAP
  Value    40

That one is a safety limit. It stops more than 40 new images being
made in a day, so nobody can run up your Google bill. Old previews
still open instantly and cost nothing.

Then click  Restart this Space  so the secrets take effect.


--------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5 - Your link
--------------------------------------------------------------
Click the  App  tab. Your link is in the address bar and looks like:

    https://huggingface.co/spaces/YOURNAME/moddex

Open it on your phone. It asks for a username and password.

    Username    anything you like, for example  shop
    Password    the PIN you set in step 4

Save that link. That is your tool, online, from anywhere.


==============================================================
  THINGS YOU SHOULD KNOW
==============================================================

It sleeps when unused
  After about 48 hours idle the free space goes to sleep. Opening the
  link wakes it, which takes around 30 seconds. After that it is fast.
  Before a customer meeting, open the link once to wake it up.

Saved previews get cleared
  On the free plan, stored images are wiped whenever the space restarts.
  The tool still works perfectly - it just has to make those previews
  again, which uses your Google credit. Anything important, save with
  the SAVE PNG button.

Your phone screen
  The layout was built for a big screen. It works on a phone but feels
  tight, especially the Studio. Ask me and I will make a phone layout.

The keys inside the build
  Your Google keys are still inside these files. On a Private space only
  you can see them, which is why Private matters. They should still be
  replaced - ask me about that when you are ready.


==============================================================
  IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
==============================================================

Build failed
  The Dockerfile probably is not at the top level. In the Files tab you
  should see Dockerfile, ModStudio.py and the ui folder listed directly.
  If you instead see a single ModStudio folder, open it, and re-upload
  the contents one level up.

It never asks for a password
  The MODDEX_PIN secret is missing or the space was not restarted.
  Redo step 4. Check the Logs tab - it prints a warning in capitals
  when there is no PIN.

Blank or stuck page
  Click Logs on the space page. The last few lines say what happened.
  Paste them to me and I will read them.
